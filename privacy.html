Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Overview

Wealthfolio is a personal finance application. This policy explains what information the app may access, why it is used, and how it is handled.

2. Google Account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, Wealthfolio may receive basic account information made available through Google OAuth, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and a Google account identifier. Wealthfolio does not receive or store your Google password.

3. Google Drive application data

If cloud backup or sync is enabled, Wealthfolio may request permission to use Google Drive application data storage. This access is intended only for Wealthfolio's own app data used for backup and synchronization. The app is not intended to browse or read unrelated personal files in your Google Drive.

4. Financial information

Information you enter into Wealthfolio may include income, expenses, assets, investments, portfolios, deposits, goals, tax-related information, transaction notes, and related settings. This information is used to provide the app's finance tracking features.

5. Local storage and sync

Wealthfolio is designed to keep a local copy of your app data on your device. If you enable cloud sync, the app may synchronize Wealthfolio data between your devices using the cloud provider you connect.

6. Data sharing

Wealthfolio does not sell your personal or financial information. Data is only shared with service providers as required to provide features you explicitly use, such as Google authentication and Google Drive app-data backup.

7. Data deletion

You may delete data from within Wealthfolio, remove local app data from your device, or revoke Wealthfolio's Google Account access from your Google Account security settings. If cloud backup data exists, you may also remove the app's stored data from the connected cloud service.

8. Security

Wealthfolio is designed to use OAuth rather than collecting your Google password. You should keep your device and Google Account secure and enable multi-factor authentication where available.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact wealthfoliobc@gmail.com.

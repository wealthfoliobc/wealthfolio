Private by design

Your financial data is handled for the purpose of operating the app and syncing your own Wealthfolio data.

Google sign-in

Use your Google Account to sign in securely. Wealthfolio never asks for or stores your Google password.

Cloud backup

When enabled, Wealthfolio can store its own app data in your Google Drive application data area for backup and device sync.
